# Haram navigation policy

The public policy is loaded before mounting from `/api/v1/navigation-settings`, validated, and cached for offline use. The defaults disable GPS, warn at a route discrepancy greater than 20 m (`snap_distance_m`), and use a 3 m arrival radius. Offline or malformed responses retain the last valid policy. Settings are edited in the existing administrator settings dialog and take effect on the next application load.

A verified QR or a selected origin anchors the raw pedestrian dead-reckoned position. An explicit GPS endpoint selection observes fresh samples for up to 30 seconds and accepts the first that meets the configured accuracy limit, capped at 25 m. The map shows the reported radius on the endpoint floor and keeps the endpoint unchanged if acquisition fails. During Haram guidance, DR uses the selected origin and does not request or consume new GPS fixes. Detected walking steps advance the display; only steps with a valid heading update the raw position. Wheelchair and vehicle modes do not convert phone motion into walking steps.

Display progress follows the server line by detected step distance. The marker and guide image keep the last position on that line during same-floor step changes. The advisory measures the distance from the raw dead-reckoned position to the corresponding section of the line, bounded by recent along-route progress on the active segment. Crossing the configured distance shows a message below the top controls for four seconds and a small badge above the QR button. Tapping the badge shows the message again. The marker and step-based guidance continue. The badge and message clear when a fresh estimate returns within the limit or a successful QR correction re-anchors the trip. The discrepancy is a geometric estimate; it cannot reveal every position error without an independent reference.

Live guidance finishes when the tracked progress reaches the endpoint on the final segment and the destination floor is active. The guide displays the localized arrival message, the existing map marker turns green with a check, and enabled speech announces arrival once. Walking progress can reach the endpoint with missing heading or an offset raw estimate. During walking guidance without GPS, an escalator with a valid server duration and landings changes floors automatically. Timing starts when tracked progress reaches the entrance, freezes during a pause, and guides from the server exit after the configured duration. Other transfers use the existing floor confirmation. The policy payload retains legacy arrival settings for compatibility with earlier clients.

Pausing stores the raw position, route progress and last measured discrepancy. Without a new QR, resuming requires the user to confirm they are still at the pause location. Floor transfer remains an explicit user action. QR correction preserves the existing camera and cancellation behavior.

Validation covers motion-event navigation without GPS, arrival with missing or biased heading, progress after a distance warning, both panel states, pause/resume, settings persistence, hairpin projection, live completion on the destination floor and administrator-enabled GPS scenarios. Physical device accuracy has not been measured by these automated tests.
